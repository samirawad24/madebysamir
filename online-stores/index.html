How working together goes.

  1. Talk

    A call about your business, your customers, and what you want more of: calls, bookings, or orders.

  2. Plan

    You see the direction before I build anything. We adjust it together until it fits.

  3. Build

    I build it mobile-first, with SEO set up from the first page.

  4. Test

    I check it on real phones, tune the speed, and tighten the copy.

  5. Launch

    Your site goes live on your domain, connected to Google.

What it costs.

The price depends on how many products you sell and how your customers pay.

After our first call I send you a written proposal with the price, the deposit, and the payment schedule. I start work once you approve it. Store platforms and payment processors charge their own fees, and they bill you directly.

Questions about online stores.

What do I need to get started?

Your product list with prices, your logo, and product photos if you have them. If something is missing, tell me on our first call and we plan around it.

Can you set up wholesale orders?

Yes. For Right Chews Foods I built a wholesale form that sends each request to their business inbox.

Will my store work on phones?

Yes. I build for the phone first and test the store on real phones before it goes live.

Can my store be in English and Spanish?

Yes. I speak both and build sites with a version of each page in each language.

Do you work with businesses outside Orlando?

Yes. I'm based in Orlando and work with businesses across the US over video calls and email.

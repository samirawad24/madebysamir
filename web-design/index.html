How working together goes.

  1. Talk

    A call about your business, your customers, and what you want more of: calls, bookings, or orders.

  2. Plan

    You see the direction before I build anything. We adjust it together until it fits.

  3. Build

    I build it mobile-first, with SEO set up from the first page.

  4. Test

    I check it on real phones, tune the speed, and tighten the copy.

  5. Launch

    Your site goes live on your domain, connected to Google.

What it costs.

The price depends on how many pages you need and what the site has to do, like take bookings or sell products.

After our first call I send you a written proposal with the price, the deposit, and the payment schedule. I start work once you approve it. Domain names and software subscriptions are billed to you by those companies.

Questions about websites.

Can you redesign my current website?

Yes. I review what you have first. If it needs fixes like speed, structure, or better words, I make them. If it isn't mobile-friendly or doesn't show up on Google, rebuilding is often faster and cheaper than patching.

Do you write the text for my site?

Yes. I ask about your business on our first call and write the copy from your answers. You see it before anything goes live.

Can you use the domain I already own?

Yes. I connect the domain you have to the new site. If you don't have one, I help you pick and register it, and the registrar bills you directly.

Can my site be in English and Spanish?

Yes. I speak both. For career coach Ana Prato I built gethiredprogram.com with an English and a Spanish version of each page.

Do you work with businesses outside Orlando?

Yes. I'm based in Orlando and work with businesses across the US over video calls and email.

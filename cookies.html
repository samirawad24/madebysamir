Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This site does not use cookies

madebysamir.com sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, advertising pixels, session recording, or social media trackers, and it loads no third-party embeds that could set cookies of their own.

The one thing your browser stores

The site saves a single item in your browser's session storage, named mbs-intro with the value 1. It tells the site you've already seen the opening animation, so it doesn't replay each time you reload the page in the same tab. It holds no personal information, is never sent to me or anyone else, and your browser deletes it when you close the tab.

The contact form

When you send the contact form, your browser sends your entries to FormSubmit, which forwards them to my email. That request doesn't include cookies. The Privacy Policy covers what happens to that information.

Why there's no cookie banner

Cookie consent banners exist so you can say yes or no to optional tracking. This site doesn't do any, so there's nothing to consent to. If that ever changes, I'll update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
